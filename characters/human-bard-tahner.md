## 🎭 Persona & Roleplay Notes
* **Voice & Demeanor:** Deep, weathered rasp laced with constant impatience and gruff superiority. He carries himself like a weary, unimpressed patriarch who believes he is far above the party's petty squabbles.
* **The "Chosen One" Complex:** Truly believes he is destined for cosmic greatness and superior to everyone around him—especially the party. Considers it an act of profound charity that he travels with them at all.
* **Incessant Heckler:** Relentlessly practices mockery at every waking opportunity, whether weaving magic or simply commenting on camp chores. If an ally misses an attack in combat, Tânher will immediately point it out, question their competence, and explain how poorly they performed.
* **Evolution into "The Elder":** Originally joined the company in his middle years as a younger tribal skald. After leaping into an erratic magical portal, he vanished—only to emerge aged decades, hair snow-white, temperament sourer, and ego doubled (much like "Tânher the White").
