**Player:** Pantral Y Galdas

---

## 🎭 Persona & Mannerisms

* **Dramatic Introductions:** Never merely waves; performs a sweeping, low bow upon meeting anyone new and recites his full name with grand ceremony.
* **Mannerisms:** Constantly and immaculately twirls the tips of his dark brown mustache when scheming, listening, or basking in applause.
* **Voice & Accent:** Speaks Common with a thick, expressive French accent—delivering dialogue with melodic inflection and theatrical pauses.
* **Mental Makeup:** 
  * **High Intelligence:** Sharp tactical thinker, brilliant arcane grasp, quick study of complex patterns and magical lore.
  * **Low Wisdom:** Impulsive, overly confident in the spotlight, prone to leaping headfirst into questionable situations for the sake of the drama.
* **Core Drive & Vow:** Born to enslaved halfling parents in a realm where halflings suffered systemic bondage; now an orphan. He carries deep, righteous fury toward slavery, cruelty, and the exploitation of halflings, never hesitating to fight on their behalf.

## ⚔️ Combat Gear, Magic & Tactics

* **Infernal Shortbow (Slung on Back):** A custom magical recurve bow crafted from the harvested horns, sinew, and guts of a slain horned demon. Fiendish, dark horn curves with abyssal textures.
* **Shortsword of Wounding (Worn at Hip):** Worn in a decorative scabbard; inflicts jagged, necrotic cuts that refuse to close.
* **Frost-Sense Dagger (Sheathed at Waist):** A magic dagger that turns freezing cold to the touch, frosting over with condensation when specific enemy types and magic draw close.
* **Lightning Affinity Spells:** Channels offensive cantrips and spells like *Shocking Grasp* and *Lightning Lure*, delivering direct electrical discharges.
* **Signature Tactic – The Living Spark:** Steps into stealth while conjuring an illusory, crackling duplicate composed of pure, arcing lightning to draw fire and strike foes down with phantom psychic damage.
* **Combat Style:** Acrobatic, evasive skirmisher—tumbling behind cover, casting illusions, and striking with sneak attacks from range or flank.
