# Galarald Aexidor (In Memoriam)

**Status:** Fallen in Battle (🕊️ Honored Ancestor)[cite: 10]  
**Ancestry & Class:** Elf Cleric of Kalex (War / Battle Domain)[cite: 10]  
**Sacred Relic & Succession:** The Amulet of Kalex (now carried by Camdyn à Adair as his Paladin holy symbol)[cite: 10, 11]  
**Final Resting Place:** Buried beneath the roots of the magical living tree overlooking the river canyon[cite: 10]  

---

## 🎭 Persona & Legacy
* **Devout Conviction & Truth:** Took his faith with absolute seriousness. Deeply religious, unyielding in his moral code, and fiercely disapproving of dishonesty, injustice, or corrupt shortcuts. Widely revered and respected by other priesthoods, holy orders, and beings of faith across the realm.
* **The "Fighting Healer":** Officially the company's designated healer and spiritual anchor, though the running party joke was that Galarald far preferred swinging in melee over casting healing spells during combat[cite: 10].
* **The First to Fall (Running Party Dynamic):** Frequently succumbed to his own frontline bravado—charging headfirst into the thick of battle only to be the very first party member knocked unconscious. Because the party's sole primary healer was down before anyone else, the rest of the company was regularly left in a frantic, spell-less scramble to stabilize and revive him mid-brawl.
* **The Living Tree Memorial:** Following the cataclysmic standoff against Jorazabal, the party reverently laid Galarald to rest beneath the sheltering canopy of the giant magical living tree on the grassy bluff above the river canyon[cite: 10]. His memory and sacrifice directly spurred Camdyn's paladin oath of protection and vengeance[cite: 10, 11].

## 📖 Story Beats & Campaign Milestones
* **Founding Pillar of the Fellowship:** Marched alongside the company through the earliest campaigns, stubbornly swinging his flail on the front lines, upholding justice, and anchoring the group through dire subterranean trials[cite: 10].
* **The "Downed Healer" Scramble:** Established a legendary table pattern of charging the vanguard and getting dropped early in encounters, forcing the party to scramble through non-magical first-aid measures to bring their cleric back to life.
* **The Final Stand against Jorazabal (The "Sauron" Clash):** Fearlessly charged into melee combat on the dark bluffs against Jorazabal—a towering, dread warlord resembling Sauron[cite: 10]. Galarald was struck down in a cataclysmic clash when Jorazabal landed a brutal critical blow with a flaming magical mace, dealing a lethal 81 damage that instantly shattered his hit points[cite: 10].
* **Vengeance via the Orb of Destruction:** In the desperate struggle immediately following Galarald's fall, the party managed to avenge their fallen cleric when Pantraal used his trickster stealth to slip an Orb of Destruction directly onto Jorazabal's person (swiftly pocketed/planted), annihilating the tyrant[cite: 10].
* **The Living Tree Burial:** Carried across the river and given a hero's burial beneath the magical living tree on the cliffs[cite: 10].
* **Living Legacy:** Camdyn took up Galarald’s sacred Amulet of Kalex, swearing his paladin oath in Galarald's honor and carrying his divine memory into every fight thereafter[cite: 10, 11].
