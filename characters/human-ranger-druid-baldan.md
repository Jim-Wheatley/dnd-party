## 🎭 Persona & Roleplay Notes
* **Demeanor & Voice:** Classic, no-nonsense Yorkshireman temperament delivered with a gruff, dry, Sean Bean-style cadence. Blunt, direct, and completely unimpressed by high-society pretenses.
* **Vices & Habits:** An enthusiastic consumer of potent wild hallucinogenic mushrooms and dangerously strong hooch—the harsher and more unrefined the brew, the better. Often found testing unfamiliar fungi on the trail.
* **The Meat Ordinance:** Hoards an absurd stockpile of rock-hard dried sausages in his packs, pockets, and bandoliers. Habitually improvises with them in tactical scenarios (e.g., measuring depths, testing floor traps, baiting predators, or feeding oozes).
* **Bond with Yer Bastid:** Fiercely, lethally protective of his hound. When Yer Bastid was once abducted into an illegal dog-fighting pit, Baldan tracked down the ringmaster and attacked on sight without uttering a single word of negotiation.
