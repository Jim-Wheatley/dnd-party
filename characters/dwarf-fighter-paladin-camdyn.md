## 🎭 Persona & Roleplay Notes
* **Voice & Demeanor:** Speaks with a low, gravelly rumble; strictly stern, laconic, and authoritative. Carries an uncompromising, immovable dwarven presence.
* **Fearless Aggression:** Completely devoid of self-preservation in combat; will charge down and pursue any enemy heedless of his own safety to hold the line or break a foe.
* **Deep Stonecunning:** An expert in stone masonry, cavern dynamics, and ancient subterranean architecture. Habitually inspects stone seams, secret hinges, and masonry weak points to decipher dungeons and traps.
* **Distrust of "Foul Magic":** Holds an old-school, deep-seated dwarven skepticism toward spells and arcane trickery, dismissing them as "foul magic" and prioritizing honest steel and physical grit.
* **The Paladin’s Burden:** Following the tragic fall of their companion Galarald, Camdyn claimed Galarald's sacred relic—the Amulet of Kalex—and swore an oath to honor his legacy, reluctantly learning to channel divine power through cold steel.
* **Habits & Mannerisms:** Smokes a long-stemmed briar pipe while assessing stone formations or resting between combat skirmishes.

## 🎨 Visual Baseline (Standard Appearance)
*Use this section as the foundational description for AI image prompts and scene artwork.*

* **Build & Stature:** Mountain dwarf proportions—heavily muscled, barrel-chested, broad-shouldered, and extremely stocky with a low center of gravity. Stands chest-height to human companions like Baldan, but massive in bulk[cite: 4, 8].
* **Facial Features & Headwear:** Heavily creased, weather-beaten brow with a stern, unflinching expression; wears an ornate dwarven steel sallet/kettle helmet with engraved cheek guards and a runic nasal strip.
* **Beard, Hair & The White Streak:** Thick, magnificent beard parted and flowing over his chest armor; primarily sandy/chestnut-brown, marked prominently by a vivid **shock of pure white hair** running straight through the beard from the magical time stairs.
* **Signature Armor:** Heavy dwarven segmented steel breastplate over thick gambeson, layered scalemail spaulders on his shoulders, scarred leather arm bracers, and cross-gartered leather wraps over baggy trousers.
* **Holy Symbol (Amulet of Kalex):** The silver-and-stone sacred medallion of Kalex taken from Galarald, strapped over his armor plate or wrapped along his gauntlet.
* **Visual Magic & Divine Flourish:** Divine power manifests with a dense, smoldering heat—golden-white embers, heat mirages, and molten cracks tracing along steel rather than ethereal floating sigils.

## ⚔️ Combat Gear, Armament & The Cursed Shield
* **Flaming Longsword (Primary Melee):** A balanced, mastercrafted dwarven steel longsword that ignites with roaring magical fire along its blade when engaged in battle.
* **Quick-Toss Handaxes:** A battery of balanced dwarven throwing axes hung from back leather frogs and his belt, thrown in fluid succession while charging into melee range[cite: 8].
* **The "Magnet" Cursed Shield:** A sturdy dwarven shield cursed with missile attraction. 
  * *The Curse:* Whenever a ranged attack targets anyone within 5 feet of Camdyn, the projectile's trajectory is magically warped mid-flight and redirected straight into Camdyn instead.
  * *The Party Gaffe:* While Camdyn took it believing he would intercept enemy arrows meant for his squishier allies, in practice it means the moment he locks an enemy into melee, **no one in the party can shoot that enemy without the shot snapping directly into Camdyn**. The party routinely forgets this mid-battle, resulting in Baldan or Pantraal accidentally pinging arrows into Camdyn's back or shield.
* **Long-Stemmed Pipe:** Carried in hand or tucked into his pack, filled with pungent dwarven pipeweed.
* **Tactics:** Aggressive frontline lockdown—hurls axes at midrange, closes distance, ignites the flaming sword, and tanks every missile in a five-foot radius.

## 📖 Story Beats & Character Growth
* **The Amphitheater Concussion:** Impulsively splashed water from the mysterious dark pool straight into the voiceless NPC's face to force a reaction; knocked senseless and deafened by the concussive backlash alongside Baldan before Pantraal saved the ritual[cite: 5].
* **The Magical Time Stairs:** Tested the temporal staircase, withstanding a surge of magical aging that permanently streaked a bolt of his beard pure white.
* **The Fall of Galarald:** Claimed the fallen companion's sacred Amulet of Kalex and swore his oath as a Paladin, balancing his innate disdain for magic with divine duty[cite: 10].
* **The Friendly Fire Incidents:** Multiple instances of the party forgetting the shield's curse during close-quarters brawls and accidentally shooting Camdyn instead of the monster.

## 🩹 Physical Changes, Scars & Relics
* **Temporal White Beard Streak:** A vivid, permanent white stripe cutting down through his dark sandy beard, earned on the magical time stairs.
* **The Amulet of Kalex:** The holy relic claimed from Galarald's body, serving as his divine focus.
* **Shield Denting / Arrow Nick Scars:** Numerous arrow dings and bolt scratches across his armor plate and shield rim courtesy of both enemies and well-meaning allies[cite: 8].
* **Amphitheater Tinnitus:** Residual ringing in his ears from the concussive shockwave at the cliffside pool[cite: 8].
