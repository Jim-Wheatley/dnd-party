## 🎭 Persona & Roleplay Notes
* **Deep Arcane Wisdom & Polyglot:** Deeply knowledgeable in all facets of the arcane, planes, and esoteric lore. Possesses an extraordinary gift for deciphering and speaking almost any language, dialect, or ancient script the company encounters.
* **The Party’s Living Chronicle:** Endowed with a near-photographic memory for every individual the party has ever met, including complex webs of alliances, rivalries, and bloodlines. Invariably the first to recognize a familiar crest, name, or hidden connection to an earlier encounter.
* **Extraplanar Sight & Keen Perception:** Having been pulled into other planes and dimensions throughout his arcane journey, Elios perceives reality with an eerie extra layer of sensory insight. Sharp, watchful, and intuitive, he is almost always the first party member to sense when something is fundamentally wrong or when an ambush is brewing.
* **Voice & Demeanor:** Measured, tranquil, and enigmatic[cite: 10]. He speaks with quiet conviction and authority, carrying the calm steadiness of someone who has stared into alien planes and returned unbroken.

## ⚔️ Combat Gear, Spells & Unorthodox Tactics
* **Deadly Blasting Artillery:** Extremely lethal at range, devastating enemy formations with searing *Fireballs* and piercing beams of *Eldritch Blast*[cite: 10].

Frequently deploys short-range teleportation (such as *Misty Step* and *Dimension Door*) to instantly displace allies—most notably teleporting Camdyn directly into prime choke points so the dwarf can lock down the enemy frontline as tank[cite: 10, 11].
